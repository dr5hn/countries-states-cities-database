## What this release changes

v5.1.0 is a regular release. It corrects facts about existing records and adds nothing, removes nothing and changes no
id. Every id in v5.0.0 means the same entity in v5.1.0, so you can update stored rows by id. See the
[data versioning contract](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/465beaae5374f84b261e2562f8fca29c089540d9/DATA_VERSIONING.md).

## Populations

Since v5.0.0 every population we add or correct comes from the national statistics office, carries its reference
year in `population_year`, and is bound to the place the row denotes: a town no longer carries its district's or
municipality's total, and a district no longer carries its main town's. 58.6% of cities now carry a population year,
up from 42.4% in v5.0.0.

## Types and levels

`level` is the depth in each country's official hierarchy, with the same meaning in states, counties and cities.
China's city rows now follow it for 2,019 rows (prefectures at 2, county-level units at 3), and 968 Philippine cities
and municipalities get their level and type from their PSGC code. Types follow
[TYPE_FIELD.md](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/465beaae5374f84b261e2562f8fca29c089540d9/TYPE_FIELD.md), which gains
sections for the United States, Uruguay, Germany, Canada, Ukraine, Australia, Portugal and China. In Guangdong, 99
districts, counties and townships get their parent unit as `parent_id`, and 5 rows their Chinese name, checked against
the Ministry of Civil Affairs place-names register (contributed in #2108).

## Time zones

2,374 cities take the IANA zone that contains their own point (timezone-boundary-builder 2026e, ODbL), for example
Brazil's north-east off São Paulo time, Puerto Rico's towns on Atlantic time, Western Australian shires on Perth time
and Russian Far East towns off Moscow time. Every country keeps its own zone name: Dutch cities stay
`Europe/Amsterdam` even though tzdata now links it to `Europe/Brussels`.

Manitoba stays on UTC−5 all year from 31 October 2026 (tzdata 2026e), so Canada's `America/Winnipeg` and
`America/Rainy_River` entries in `countries.json` now give UTC−05:00 (EST) as their standard offset.

## Wikidata items

Items are added where a row's own official code or own GeoNames feature proves its item, and cleared where an item
belonged to another place (for example a district carrying its town's item). Items shared by two city rows were
reviewed: wrong ones are cleared, and pairs that are the same place stored twice are listed for the next major.

## What stays the same

No ids, names, countries, states or postcodes change meaning. Rows in disputed or special territories were left as
they were, with one exception: Sevastopol's row takes the category Ukraine's KATOTTH codifier gives it, a city with special
status, like Kyiv. Every change is traced, record by record, in the ledgers under
[`.github/fixes-docs/ledgers/`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/465beaae5374f84b261e2562f8fca29c089540d9/.github/fixes-docs/ledgers).
