# Countries States Cities Database v6.0.0

v6.0.0 is a major release (data contract 6). It ships every fix merged since v5.2.0, about 10,900 city rows,
and changes the documented settlement filter for the United States, Romania and Poland so that municipal
territories are no longer counted as settlements. No row is removed, moved to another table or renumbered, and
every stored id stays valid.

Counts compare the latest published download,
[v5.2.0-export.1](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/releases/tag/v5.2.0-export.1)
(source `9b255c45`), with this release's source (`581b1ca5`).

## This release is not marked "latest"

v6.0.0 and its export are published without the **latest** flag, so anything that downloads from
`releases/latest`, including the `countrystatecity` npm and PyPI packages, the export tool and the website's
download links, keeps receiving v5.2.0-export.1 for now. Download v6 by its tag. It becomes latest later, once
those consumers have adopted the new settlement filter; that move will be announced.

## Settlement filter: what to change

If you list only settlements (cities, towns, villages) with the filter in [TYPE_FIELD.md](../../TYPE_FIELD.md),
update it together with the data. The full query, with null-type handling and the other country rules, is in
TYPE_FIELD.md; these are the changed clauses.

```sql
-- contract 5
AND (country_code NOT IN ('CU', 'MX', 'GR', 'VN') OR type IS NULL OR type <> 'municipality')
-- contract 6
AND (country_code NOT IN ('CU', 'MX', 'GR', 'VN', 'RO', 'US') OR type IS NULL OR type <> 'municipality')
AND (country_code <> 'PL' OR type IS NULL OR type NOT IN ('municipality', 'adm2', 'adm3'))
```

```js
// contract 5
!(['CU', 'MX', 'GR', 'VN'].includes(c.country_code) && c.type === 'municipality')
// contract 6
!(['CU', 'MX', 'GR', 'VN', 'RO', 'US'].includes(c.country_code) && c.type === 'municipality')
  && !(c.country_code === 'PL' && ['municipality', 'adm2', 'adm3'].includes(c.type))
```

With the v5.2.0 data and filter you got 150,728 rows; with this release's data and filter you get 146,300.

- 4,440 rows you had before leave the result: 2,656 Romanian comune, 1,628 Polish gminy and 92 Polish rows not
  yet reviewed (`adm2`, `adm3`), 62 US municipality rows and the León and Ojinaga municipio rows (their cities
  keep their own rows, 71668 and 71964).
- 12 rows join: 11 new Romanian towns and cities and the Amealco seat town, now typed as the town it is.
- Of the 6,630 new rows, 6,619 are territories and stay out of the result.

TYPE_FIELD.md's Poland section already told you to leave these rows out, and its Romania section now says the
same; the SQL and JavaScript examples match both. Two known limits remain until v7: 1,478 older US territorial towns keep their published `town / town`
type and still appear in the result (listed by id in `.github/fixes-docs/ledgers/v6_contract.sources.json`), and
Bethel Park, PA (112287), a real town typed `municipality` for its Pennsylvania legal class, drops out. A
territory's population includes its places, so don't add the two together.

## Other corrections

- Parent links: 3,406 cities gain or correct their direct parent (US 1,594, Romania 556, Australia 412, Mexico
  362, Italy 337, China 86, Nigeria 36, Netherlands 23); 64 German Gemeinden gain their Landkreis.
- Levels: 2,000 rows, mostly US places under their new townships, Taiwanese districts, Scottish localities and
  Chinese prefecture groups.
- Wikidata items: 428 rows get their own item, including 273 French communes, 129 German Gemeinden, 12 Malaysian
  places and 10 Swiss municipalities.
- Types: own national categories for Moroccan communes, Serbian opštine, Taiwanese districts and sampled rows
  elsewhere; León and Ojinaga become their municipio, Amealco returns to its seat town.
- Points: 13 rows move to their own place, mostly Selangor towns whose imported points were 10 to 50 km off.

Each change is recorded per row in `.github/fixes-docs/ledgers/`, with sources and before and after values.

## Upgrading

Pin the export tag and source commit, then refresh the data and the settlement query together. The export's
migration archive lists added ids and changed fields from v5.2.0; check its `from` and `to` commits and checksums.
Removals, merged duplicates and any other filter change wait for v7.
