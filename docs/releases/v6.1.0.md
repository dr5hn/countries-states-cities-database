# Countries States Cities Database v6.1.0

v6.1.0 is a minor release under data contract 6, the same contract as v6.0.0. It adds 8,468 city rows and corrects
44, with no row removed, moved to another table or renumbered, and no change to the documented settlement filter.
Every stored id stays valid.

Counts compare the v6.0.0 download,
[v6.0.0-export.1](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/releases/tag/v6.0.0-export.1)
(source [`e1c81571`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/e1c8157142c0db6e405e4b98f8230ab69e01d3ad)),
with this release's source, tag [`v6.1.0`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/v6.1.0).

## This release is not marked "latest"

Like v6.0.0, v6.1.0 and its export are published without the **latest** flag. Anything that downloads from
`releases/latest`, including the `countrystatecity` npm and PyPI packages, the export tool and the website's download
links, keeps receiving v5.2.0-export.1 for now. Download v6.1.0 by its tag.

## New rows

| Country | Added | What they are |
|---|---:|---|
| United States | 7,923 | Functioning townships and towns in eight more states: MN 1,740, ND 1,292, KS 1,235, WI 1,172, IN 999, SD 889, NE 315, MO 281. All `municipality`, with `type_local` `township`, or `town` for Wisconsin's civil towns; 2020 Census points and populations. |
| Poland | 455 | Seat villages and towns of gminy that had no row of their own, each bound by its own TERYT SIMC code inside its gmina: 380 `village`, 67 `city`, 8 `locality`. |
| Romania | 88 | Seat villages of comune that had no row of their own, each bound by its own SIRUTA code (INS 2026). |
| Mexico | 2 | The seat towns Poncitlán and Tototlán, with their own INEGI locality codes. |

Points come only from open sources: US Census (public domain), Wikidata (CC0), GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and, for one
Polish village, OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Populations are each place's own dated figure; where none exists the field is
empty, never a parent total.

## Settlement filter

The rules in [TYPE_FIELD.md](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/v6.1.0/TYPE_FIELD.md#filtering-to-genuine-settlements)
are unchanged. With v6.0.0's data you got 150,879 rows; with this release's data you get 151,423.

- 545 rows join: the 455 Polish and 88 Romanian seat places, and Poncitlán and Tototlán.
- 1 row leaves: San Bartolo Coyotepec 73401 is now typed as the municipio it is, and the filter leaves Mexican
  municipal territories out. Its seat town keeps its own row, 73402.
- The 7,923 US townships and towns stay out, under contract 6's US rule. A township's population includes the places
  inside it, so don't add the two together.

Romanian comune and Polish gminy still stay in the result, as v6.0.0 described. The seat rows added here are what v7
needs before those territories can be left out without dropping the places they serve.

## Other corrections

- 41 US places now sit under the township or town that contains them, at level 4, for example Arlington, NE, and
  Dakota Dunes, SD. Each stays in the settlement result.
- Two older Polish rows are proved to be gmina seats and corrected in place instead of being added again: 88468 is now
  Stary Uścimów and 86384 is now Gródek. Gródek's population is now empty, because the figure it carried was the whole
  gmina's.

Each change is recorded per row in [`.github/fixes-docs/ledgers/`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/v6.1.0/.github/fixes-docs/ledgers),
with sources and before and after values.

## Known limits

These stay held until their identity is proved: Crișan, the seat of Comuna Crișan in Romania (an older row, 93733,
may denote a different place); eight Polish gmina seats whose official seat is ambiguous; one Polish seat with no
open-licence point; and Township 3 in Harper County, Kansas, whose Census boundary crosses itself. The limits listed
in v6.0.0 also remain: 1,478 older US territorial towns keep their `town / town` type, and Bethel Park, PA (112287)
is typed `municipality`.

## Upgrading

No query change is needed. Pin the export tag and its source commit, then refresh the data. The export's migration
archive lists added ids and changed fields, both as a step from v6.0.0-export.1 and cumulatively from v5.2.0; check
its `from` and `to` commits and checksums. Removals, merged duplicates and any filter change wait for v7.
