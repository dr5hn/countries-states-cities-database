# Countries States Cities Database v6.2.0

v6.2.0 is a minor release under data contract 6, the same contract as v6.0.0 and v6.1.0. It corrects facts on
3,390 existing city rows and 11 states, with no row added, removed, moved to another table or renumbered, and no
change to the documented settlement filter. Every stored id stays valid.

Counts compare the v6.1.0 download,
[v6.1.0-export.1](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/releases/tag/v6.1.0-export.1)
(source [`22dd8fba`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/22dd8fba)),
with this release's source, tag [`v6.2.0`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/v6.2.0).

## This release is not marked "latest"

Like v6.0.0 and v6.1.0, v6.2.0 and its export are published without the **latest** flag. Anything that downloads
from `releases/latest`, including the `countrystatecity` npm and PyPI packages, the export tool and the website's
download links, keeps receiving v5.2.0-export.1 for now. Download v6.2.0 by its tag.

## Wikidata items

477 Illinois townships get their own item, each matched on its own GNIS or FIPS code with its point inside the
township's Census boundary. One Mexican row changes item with its corrected name (below).

## Time zones

Palestine: 11 states and 70 cities in the West Bank move from `Asia/Gaza` to `Asia/Hebron`, the zone for the West
Bank. Bani Zeid is one of them. East Jerusalem (154002), Har Adar (57475) and Modiin Ilit (57514) keep
`Asia/Jerusalem`, because their civil time follows Israel's.

## Other corrections

- San Bartolo Coyotepec (73402, Oaxaca) is spelled correctly, with its own item and translations.
- Counties: the source files now store each county's `created_at`, `updated_at` and `flag`, so exports stop
  stamping every county with the export date. That is why all 14,058 counties show as changed: their names, codes
  and links are the same.

Each change is recorded per row in [`.github/fixes-docs/ledgers/`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/v6.2.0/.github/fixes-docs/ledgers),
with sources, before and after values, and the rows that were held and why.

## Settlement filter

The rules in [TYPE_FIELD.md](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/v6.2.0/TYPE_FIELD.md#filtering-to-genuine-settlements)
are unchanged, and so is the result: 151,423 rows with v6.1.0's data and with this release's data. No row joins or
leaves. The retyped Tokyo wards, German Gemeinden and Sassofeltrio stay in.

## Known limits

- US: 13,763 townships and towns still have no Wikidata item. Their lookup was cut short by Wikidata's rate limit
  and continues in the next release.
- Held rows stay as they are until their identity or scope is proved, and each is listed with its reason in its
  ledger: for example 210 Romanian UATs, 374 French and 898 Mexican rows without an own population, 572 German
  parts without an own local designation, and Larequille and Oppède le Vieux in France, whose items name another
  commune.
- The limits listed in v6.1.0 remain.

## Upgrading

No query change is needed. Pin the export tag and its source commit, then refresh the data. The export's migration
archive lists changed fields, both as a step from v6.1.0-export.1 and cumulatively from v5.2.0; check its `from`
and `to` commits and checksums. The release ledger records a `classification` event for each row whose type, local
type or level changed. Removals, merged duplicates and any filter change wait for v7.
