# Countries States Cities Database v5.3.0

Counts describe source commit [`bc3dfd48`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/tree/bc3dfd484154b400208d46da9bdac4a466f7afc7), compared with
[`v5.2.0`](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/releases/tag/v5.2.0).

## What this release changes

v5.3.0 is a regular release. It adds missing administrative units and corrects existing rows; it removes nothing and
changes no id. Every id in v5.2.0 means the same entity in v5.3.0. See the
[data versioning contract](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/bc3dfd484154b400208d46da9bdac4a466f7afc7/DATA_VERSIONING.md).

| Cities | 2,710 | 6,512 | 0 |
| States, counties, countries, postcodes | 0 | 0 | 0 |

60.6% of cities carry a population year, up from 59.1% in v5.2.0.

## New rows

| Country | Added | What they are | Source |
|---|---:|---|---|
| United States | 6,298 | functioning townships and towns in Pennsylvania (1,500), Illinois (1,340), Ohio (1,293), Michigan (1,214), New York (776) and New Jersey (175), with their 2020 Census count | US Census Bureau, public domain |
| Romania | 214 | communes (203), towns (8) and municipalities (3, e.g. Baia Mare) that had no row | INS SIRUTA, CC BY 4.0; Wikidata points |

Townships in the other township states (Kansas, Minnesota, the Dakotas, Wisconsin, Indiana, Missouri, Nebraska) are not
added: few places would link to them yet.

## Parent links

2,234 rows now point to their direct parent unit:

- United States: 1,594 places (villages, CDPs, boroughs and one city, Sherrill, NY) to the town or township they sit in
  (level 4), from Census 2020 place-to-county-subdivision relationships: 1,230 to the new townships, 364 to towns and
  townships that already had rows (New England, New York, New Jersey).
- Romania: 556 villages to their commune or town by SIRUTA (546 to the new units).
- Nigeria: 36 headquarters towns that were typed as their local government area are now towns (type `city`, level 3)
  under the LGA row.
- China: 48 Guangxi districts, counties and townships to their prefecture or county, from the Ministry of Civil Affairs
  register (contributed in #2149).

Separately, 64 German Gemeinden get their Landkreis as `county_id` from their official municipal key.

## Wikidata items

414 rows that are the municipality carried the item of their main village or settlement and now carry the
municipality's own item, matched by its official code: 273 French communes (INSEE), 129 German Gemeinden (AGS) and 10
Swiss municipalities (BFS). Two Nigerian towns get their own items back.

## Filtering settlements

Unit rows now appear next to the places they contain, and a unit can share its seat's name (the village of Millington,
Michigan, inside Millington Township). To list only places people live in:

- United States: `type IN ('city', 'town', 'village', 'borough', 'locality')`. Townships are `municipality` with
  `type_local` `township` or `charter township`. A New York `town` or a New Jersey township can contain villages
  (level 4, with the town or township as `parent_id`).
- Romania, Mexico, Poland, Cuba: leave out type `municipality` (in Poland also `adm2` and `adm3`). In Romania a town
  or city can appear twice, as its level-2 unit and as its level-3 locality (e.g. Bacău); a few appear only as the unit
  (e.g. Onești), so keep the level-2 `town` and `city` rows.
- A unit's population includes its settlements'; don't add the two.

Rules per country are in [TYPE_FIELD.md](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/bc3dfd484154b400208d46da9bdac4a466f7afc7/TYPE_FIELD.md), which gains a Romania section.
