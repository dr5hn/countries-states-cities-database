## What this release changes

v5.2.0 is a regular release. It adds missing places and corrects existing ones; it removes nothing and changes no id.
Every id in v5.1.0 means the same entity in v5.2.0. See the
[data versioning contract](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/29dd43bb557c88e31920640585eff7c8f78494bf/DATA_VERSIONING.md).

## Municipality rows

A municipio, gmina or local government area is a city row of type `municipality` (level 2, or level 3 for a gmina, under
its powiat), and its
towns link to it through `parent_id` (8,089 links in this release: Mexico 6,581, Poland 1,498, Cuba 10). A municipality
can share its town's name (the gmina Krobia and the town of Krobia), and its population includes its towns'.

- To list only settlements, leave out type `municipality` (in Poland also `adm2` and `adm3`).
- To avoid double counting, don't add a municipality's population to its towns'.

[TYPE_FIELD.md](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/29dd43bb557c88e31920640585eff7c8f78494bf/TYPE_FIELD.md) gains sections for Mexico, Poland and Cuba.

- Pakistan: Bhimber, Rawalakot and Vehari get their standard spelling.
